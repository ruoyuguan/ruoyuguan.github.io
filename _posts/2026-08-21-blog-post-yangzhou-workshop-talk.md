I'm happy to share that on July 31, 2026, I gave an oral presentation at the 2026 Mid-Year Workshop of the National Key R&D Program of China project on "Signal Simulation, Processing, and Verification Technologies for Space Gravitational-Wave Detection" (国家重点研发计划引力波探测专项"空间引力波探测信号仿真、处理与验证技术研究"项目2026年中交流会), held in Yangzhou, Jiangsu.

In my talk, "Galactic Binary Residuals in LISA SGWB Amplitude Inference: Source Power Concentration and Spectral Degeneracy," I presented our recent work on how the residual Galactic compact-binary foreground impacts SGWB amplitude inference with LISA through source power concentration and spectral degeneracy, based on our new preprint with my supervisor, Prof. Yan Wang.

Many thanks to the organizers for the opportunity, and to everyone who joined the session for the helpful discussions.
