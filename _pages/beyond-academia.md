Research is a central part of my life, but not the whole of it. I also spend time on endurance running, specialty coffee, photography, and videography.

## Running

I am a dedicated distance runner with experience in marathons, half marathons, and trail races. In April 2024, I completed the Hong Kong 5 Peaks – 30km Solo, finishing the 29.7 km course with 1,560 m of elevation gain in 6:36:38. My recorded trail race results are available on my [ITRA profile](https://itra.run/RunnerSpace/guan.ruoyu.5315435).

I value running as a steady practice, whether on roads or trails.

### Selected Road Race Results

- **2025 Xiantao Marathon** — Marathon, March 30, 2025; net time 4:52:51.
- **2024 Changchun Marathon** — Marathon, September 1, 2024; net time 5:12:27.
- **Standard Chartered Hong Kong Marathon 2024** — Marathon, January 21, 2024; chip time 05:02:22 ([official result](https://sporthive.com/events/s/7146031303728157696/race/7146031303728158720/bib/B1712)).
- **32nd Dalian International Marathon** — Half Marathon, May 12, 2019; net time 1:56:21. For this performance, I also earned a Level 2 certificate in the Chinese Athletics Association's grading system for mass runners (men's half marathon, 29-and-under age group).

## Specialty Coffee

I am a specialty-coffee enthusiast and former part-time barista. I enjoy preparing manual espresso, pour-over coffee, and cold brew, experimenting with different brewing methods and extraction parameters. For me, coffee is both a daily practice and an opportunity to learn more about flavor, technique, and repeatability.

## Photography and Videography

I enjoy street and documentary-style photography, capturing everyday life, city scenes, and places I visit. I mainly practice digital photography and have recently begun exploring half-frame film photography.

I also use action cameras for still photography and first-person (POV) videography, particularly when documenting travel, running, and everyday experiences. I prefer natural perspectives, ambient sound, and an unobtrusive approach to recording the world around me.
