I am a PhD Candidate in Physics (Theoretical Physics) at the School of Physics and National Gravitation Laboratory, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), supervised by Prof. Yan Wang. Since March 2026, I have been a Core Member of the LISA Consortium in the Astrophysics Working Group.

My research concerns space-based gravitational-wave astrophysics, with a focus on Galactic compact binaries—particularly double white dwarfs—and their unresolved gravitational-wave foregrounds. I study residual foreground statistics, angular anisotropy, and the implications for stochastic gravitational-wave background inference.

I have three first-author journal articles: a [Physical Review D paper]({{ '/publications/residual-galactic-binary-foreground-lisa/' | relative_url }}) accepted for publication in 2026, a [2026 JHEAp paper]({{ '/publications/grb-hurst-index/' | relative_url }}) on gamma-ray burst time series, and a [2023 A&A paper]({{ '/publications/grb-time-integrated-polarization/' | relative_url }}) on gamma-ray burst polarization. My earlier work also included X-ray and optical counterpart identification in Cygnus OB2.
