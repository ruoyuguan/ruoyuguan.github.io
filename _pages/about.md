I am a Ph.D. candidate in theoretical physics at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), affiliated with the School of Physics and the National Gravitation Laboratory, under the supervision of Prof. Yan Wang. Since March 2026, I have been a Core Member of the LISA Consortium in the Astrophysics Working Group.

My research focuses on Galactic compact binaries, particularly double white dwarfs, and their gravitational-wave foreground in the millihertz band. I study the statistical properties and angular anisotropy of this foreground, as well as the impact of residual foreground power on stochastic gravitational-wave background inference.

My research has resulted in three first-author journal papers, including a study of residual Galactic-binary foregrounds accepted for publication in [*Physical Review D* (2026)]({{ '/publications/residual-galactic-binary-foreground-lisa/' | relative_url }}). My earlier work on gamma-ray burst time-series statistics and prompt-emission polarization appeared in [*Journal of High Energy Astrophysics* (2026)]({{ '/publications/grb-hurst-index/' | relative_url }}) and [*Astronomy & Astrophysics* (2023)]({{ '/publications/grb-time-integrated-polarization/' | relative_url }}), respectively. During my M.Sc., I also investigated X-ray sources and their optical counterparts in Cygnus OB2.
