## Privacy Policy

This is a personal academic website. It does not require registration, does not have comment or contact forms, and does not collect any personal information you actively provide.

### Analytics

Optional Google Analytics 4 is **off by default**. If you enable it below, Google receives usage information to help me understand visits to this website and may set analytics cookies. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This website does not enable Google signals or advertising personalization.

Your choice is stored in this browser. You can change it here at any time. Disabling analytics stops further collection by this site's analytics integration and removes its accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. Existing data already sent to Google is not deleted by this control. Browsers that send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track keep analytics disabled.

### Local preferences and external resources

The theme preference and analytics choice use browser local storage. Pages containing equations load MathJax from jsDelivr. The talk map uses Leaflet from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those services receive the technical information needed to serve their resources when the relevant pages are visited.

### Hosting

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes; see [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
