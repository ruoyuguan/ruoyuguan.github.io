## Privacy Policy

This is a personal academic website. It does not require registration, does not have comment or contact forms, and does not collect any personal information you actively provide.

### Analytics

This site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand aggregate visitor traffic (pages visited, approximate region, referral sources, and file downloads). Google Analytics sets cookies and processes usage data on Google's servers in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). No personally identifiable information is sent to Google by this site, and the data is used solely to improve the website's content.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using a browser content blocker or the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Hosting

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may log standard technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes; see [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
